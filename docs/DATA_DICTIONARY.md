# Data dictionary

| Field or concept | Definition | Unit or rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| quarter | Consecutive quarterly index | For example 2000Q1 |
| current_account | Current-account surplus positive | USD million |
| fdi, portfolio, other | Net financial inflows positive | USD million; reserves excluded |
| valuation | Valuation-driven reserve stock change | USD million |
| reserves_open | Opening reserve stock | Positive USD million |
| gdp | Quarterly nominal GDP | Positive USD million |
| usd_inr | INR per US dollar | Positive rate |

New runnable synthetic methods demonstration and detailed research-scope documentation. Original archive remains separate.

The default example is synthetic or illustrative. Real-data inputs require their own permissions, definitions and source audit. Missing or infinite values are not evidence of a valid measured zero.
